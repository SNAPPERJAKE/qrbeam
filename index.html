    How to

    1. Open QR Beam on both devices.
    2. On the one with the file, tap Send and pick files or type some text.
    3. On the other, tap Receive and point its camera at the flashing code. It locks on by itself.
    4. Missed frames don't matter. It keeps going until it has everything, then chimes. Tap Save or Share.

    On iPhone: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. After that QR Beam opens and works in airplane mode.
