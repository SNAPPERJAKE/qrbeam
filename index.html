Move files between devices using only light.

One device flashes a stream of QR codes and the other reads them with its camera. The devices never connect. No Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or account involved.

  1. Open QR Beam on both devices.
  2. Tap Send on one and choose what to send.
  3. Tap Receive on the other and fill the box with the flashing code. Missed frames don't matter; it keeps going until it has everything.

On iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. After that QR Beam opens and works in airplane mode.
